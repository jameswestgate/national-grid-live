Privacy Policy

National Grid: Live · Last updated 3 June 2026

National Grid: Live (“the app”) is designed to respect your privacy completely. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information, and it contains no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-in, collects no personal data, and uses no analytics or advertising SDKs. We — the developer — receive no information about you or your use of the app.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (such as the chart style and appearance) and a short-lived cache of grid data are stored only on your device, using Apple’s standard on-device storage. This information never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Third-party data sources

To show the live status of Great Britain’s electricity grid, the app requests publicly available datasets directly from the following services. These are ordinary requests for public data and contain no personal information about you. Each provider operates under its own privacy policy and may, like any web service, log standard technical request information such as IP address:

  • Elexon — Insights Solution (BMRS) generation and pricing data.
  • National Energy System Operator (NESO) — Data Portal demand and embedded generation data.
  • Carbon Intensity API — a project by NESO and the University of Oxford Department of Computer Science.

The app also downloads a pre-computed historical summary file hosted on GitHub Pages; this is a static file request and collects no personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

Because the app processes no personal data, there is no personal information for us to access, correct, or delete. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at the address below.
