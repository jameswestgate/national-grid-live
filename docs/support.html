National Grid: Live

National Grid: Live shows how Great Britain’s electricity is being generated right now — the live mix of gas, wind, solar, nuclear and more, along with carbon intensity, demand and prices, plus historic trends.

Getting help

To report a problem, ask a question, or request a feature, please open an issue on the project’s GitHub page:

github.com/jameswestgate/national-grid-live/issues

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Data

The app shows publicly available open data from the Elexon Insights Solution, the National Energy System Operator (NESO) Data Portal, and the Carbon Intensity API. If a figure looks wrong, it may originate in the source data, which can be revised after publication.

Privacy

The app collects no personal data. See the privacy policy.
